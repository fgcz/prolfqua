# Statistical standards compliance: prolfqua 1.9.4

Assessment date: 2026-11-17. The selected category is **Regression and Supervised Learning** (`regression`), alongside the mandatory General standards. The package fits regressions to proteomics features, computes contrasts and uncertainty, and adjusts for multiple testing. This assessment follows the requested `stats-standards-compliance` skill and the follow-up review with `package-standards` and `stats-standards`. No other category was requested or scored in this pass.

The canonical [checklist](srr-standards.md) contains 68 General and 48 Regression standards, downloaded through srr 1.1.0.9000 against standards version 0.3.0. Full source texts are [General](general.html) and [Regression](regression.html), obtained from the [rOpenSci review book](https://github.com/ropensci/statistical-software-review-book/tree/main/standards). All 116 IDs were assessed individually; the [assessment TSV](standards-assessment.tsv) records current status, potential verdict, evidence, gaps and tag location.

## Current implementation and potential feasibility

Current **met** means implemented and supported by the inspected code, documentation and tests. Only fully met requirements receive `@srrstats`. **Partial** and **not met** both retain `@srrstatsTODO`, with current evidence and the remaining work stated beside the relevant code. **NA** has an explicit reason in the central `NA_standards` block. This corrects the original partial-tag convention: automated srr counts now match the assessment of current compliance.

The skill uses a different, prospective verdict: **Y** includes both implemented requirements and unfinished work achievable within the current architecture; **N** means a fundamental scope conflict; **NA** is outside the present workflow. Potential scores are always **Y / (Y + N + NA)**, including NA in the denominator. A potential Y does not mean the feature exists, the work is small, or submission is ready.

Implemented evidence includes validated formulas, categorical predictor/loss-of-information documentation, and a rendered [formula-to-design article](../vignettes/articles/LowLevelAPI.html) with asserted one-factor and interaction contrasts (RE1.0–RE1.3a). Strategies expose model specifications before fitting (RE4.1); Model, ModelLimma and ModelFirth retain fitted results (RE4.0), and their explicit coefficient accessors satisfy RE4.2. The standards explicitly allow equivalent accessors, so lack of an S3 generic alone is not treated as failure. RE4.8 is **partial**: facade `$.lfqdata` aliases the caller's mutable object. A reproduction changed all reported response values by 100 after changing the caller's abundances, while the limma fit retained no full response matrix. Per-subject Model objects retain their nested data, but fitted response values and metadata need a reliable snapshot across backends.

The [runtime data](runtime.tsv), [reproduction script](benchmark-runtime.R) and [environment](runtime-session.txt) support RE5.0. With 12 samples and three peptides per protein for nested models, facade-construction timings for 500/1000/2000 proteins were 0.173/0.288/0.592 s for lm, 0.077/0.043/0.050 s for limma and 7.902/15.314/29.667 s for lmer_nested. These are single illustrative runs with fixed design and no missing responses; lazy contrast extraction is excluded, and some mixed-model fits emitted convergence warnings. No universal runtime guarantee or inference-quality claim follows from them.

Feasible work still unfinished includes explicit NA/NaN/Inf and missing-predictor policies, preprocessing diagnostics for dependence, uniform per-protein convergence metadata and controls, coefficient intervals and uniform formula/nobs/covariance/fitted/residual access, transformation provenance, useful object print/summary/default plotting, fitted-response intervals, and broader backend/reference/recovery tests. Much information already exists in inner fits, but matrix and detection backends need consistent semantics before adding public accessors.

Regression NA reasons: **RE4.14** forecast errors, **RE4.15** forecast-error growth with horizon, **RE6.3** forecast plots and **RE7.4** forecast tests do not apply because prolfqua offers designed-experiment contrast inference and no public forecasting workflow. Adding forecasting would require reassessing those declarations. **RE4.16 is unmet, with potential Y**: the standard explicitly concerns prediction for new categorical groups independently of forecasting. Absence of a prediction API does not make this requirement inapplicable. No entire Regression subsection is NA.

## Follow-up fixes and verification

The 2026-11-17 review exposed two selector defects. ANOVA validation now preserves all supported GLM test choices (`F`, `Chisq`, `LRT`, `Rao`, `Cp`) through the same constructor check, and the extractor reports the correct p-value column (none for `Cp`). Scalar selectors reject full choice vectors instead of allowing rlang's default-vector convention to silently choose the first entry. Tests reproduce these failures and compare ANOVA tables with `stats::anova`. The Firth histogram retains its effective default of `p.value`.

G2.3/G2.3a remain met after those fixes and their regression tests. RE4.8 was downgraded to partial, RE4.16 changed from NA to unmet, and all partial claims now remain TODOs. Internal parameter descriptions were reviewed for misleading shared wording and corrected, including response values versus column names, matrix lists, palette size, quadrature size and annotation coordinates.

Completion of the original plan and current executable results are recorded in [verification](verification.html). The broader rOpenSci review is separate from completion of this groundwork plan. Historical runtime measurements and the rendered [Under the hood](../vignettes/articles/LowLevelAPI.html) and [Modelling methods](../vignettes/ModellingMethods.html) examples remain available.

`autotest` mutation testing was not repeated: the recorded 2026-11-16 investigation found 4,388 traces for 12 functions, about 6 GB on disk and 66 GB in RAM on a 48 GB machine; two runs were killed. A prior traced test run passed on 1.9.3 with development typetracer fixes. That historical result is not an autotest mutation pass for this revision. Targeted input-validation tests supply executable evidence, and the resource limitation must be disclosed for review. Details are preserved in the [workspace investigation](../../TODO/prolfqua/TODO_ropensci_compliance.md#6-autotest).
