Summary fit counts still do not close per-subject convergence diagnostics under RE4.7.

The [runtime data](runtime.tsv), [reproduction script](benchmark-runtime.R) and [environment](runtime-session.txt) support RE5.0. With 12 samples and three peptides per protein for nested models, facade-construction timings for 500/1000/2000 proteins were 0.173/0.288/0.592 s for lm, 0.077/0.043/0.050 s for limma and 7.902/15.314/29.667 s for lmer_nested. These are single illustrative runs with fixed design and no missing responses; lazy contrast extraction is excluded, and some mixed-model fits emitted convergence warnings. No universal runtime guarantee or inference-quality claim follows from them.

Feasible work still unfinished includes explicit NA/NaN/Inf and missing-predictor policies, preprocessing diagnostics for dependence, uniform per-protein convergence metadata and controls, covariance estimation controls and broader backend/reference/recovery tests. Fitted values and residuals deliberately include observed responses only; a subject-level imputation flag identifies rescued fits without presenting LOD-filled rows as observations.
